# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First release of SuperHub-v3: SuperHub with a retro handheld console home screen. Original pixel-art screen with the clock, date, week and battery; nine assignable console buttons with optional names; a Bookshelf page with three shelves of up to five covers (recent notes, recent reading, favourites or starred pages); a cog for SuperHub's settings and a close button above the screen, like the other SuperHubs.

Based on SuperDashboard by AgP42; siblings: https://github.com/weedwhitesandwine/SuperHub and https://github.com/weedwhitesandwine/SuperHub-v2.
